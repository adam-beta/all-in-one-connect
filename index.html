Lead Capture & Follow-Up Automation - Available Now!

Our system collects customer details the moment they reach out—then instantly sends them a friendly auto-reply to confirm their message was received. At the same time, you get a notification with their full information by email. All leads are automatically logged into a spreadsheet for easy tracking. An hour later, the system sends you a follow-up reminder via email—helping you stay on top of every lead and respond while interest is still high. It’s a smart, hands-free way to boost follow-up and close more deals.

Automated Quote Follow-Ups - Coming Soon

We set up an automated system that instantly replies whenever someone requests a quote, then sends follow-up reminders at 24 and 72 hours if they don’t respond. This keeps your business top-of-mind and turns more leads into paying customers. All messages are sent smoothly through Gmail or SMS, so you save time and never miss an opportunity. It’s a powerful, easy way to grow your client base and close more jobs—helping your business thrive without extra effort on your part.

Google Review Booster - Coming Soon

After you complete a job, an automated text or email is sent asking for a Google review with your direct link. If they don’t respond, follow-up reminders are sent to encourage reviews. More positive reviews boost your ranking and attract more clients. The entire process runs smoothly through Gmail or SMS, saving you time while building your online reputation effortlessly. It’s an easy, effective way to grow your business and get noticed by more customers.
